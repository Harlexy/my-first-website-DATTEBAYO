What is algebra?

Algebra is a way to use letters (like x or y) to stand for numbers. We write expressions and equations to model problems and then solve them to find the unknown value.

Variable

A symbol (like x) that represents an unknown number.

Expression

A combination of numbers, variables, and operations (e.g., 2x + 3).

Equation

A statement that two expressions are equal (e.g., 2x + 3 = 11).

How to solve a simple equation

  1. Start: 2x + 3 = 11
  2. Subtract 3 from both sides: 2x = 8
  3. Divide both sides by 2: x = 4

Key idea: whatever you do to one side, do to the other side to keep the equality.
